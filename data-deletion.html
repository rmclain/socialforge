User Data Deletion Instructions

SocialForge is a local-first macOS application with a stateless OAuth relay. We do not store user accounts, posts, media, or OAuth tokens on any SocialForge server — all data resides exclusively on your own Mac.

1. Disconnect an Account in SocialForge

  1. Open SocialForge on your Mac and navigate to Connections › Social Accounts.
  2. Open the account menu (…) for the connected Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Pinterest, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, or YouTube account and choose Disconnect.
  3. SocialForge immediately removes the associated access and refresh tokens from your local macOS Keychain.

2. Revoke Access on the Social Platform

  • Facebook & Instagram: Visit Facebook Settings & Privacy › Settings › Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites) and remove SocialForge.
  • Threads: Open Threads Settings › Account › Website permissions and remove SocialForge.
  • Pinterest: Open Pinterest Settings › Security › Connected apps and revoke access.
  • LinkedIn: Open LinkedIn Settings › Data privacy › Permitted services and remove SocialForge.
  • Google / YouTube: Visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove SocialForge.
  • X & TikTok: Revoke SocialForge under Connected Apps in your account security settings.

3. Delete All Local Application Data

  1. Quit SocialForge.
  2. Move ~/Library/Application Support/SocialForge/ and ~/Library/Caches/com.socialforge.app/ to the Trash.
  3. Remove any remaining com.socialforge.app entries in macOS Keychain Access.

Need Confirmation?

Because we store zero remote user data, disconnecting and deleting local files removes 100% of your data immediately. If you would like written confirmation or assistance, email mclainr@gmail.com.
